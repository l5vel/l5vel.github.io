Autonomous ground resupply

A-MPEP

L5VEL's autonomy stack integrated on the tracked Multi-Purpose Expeditionary Platform. It accepts a route, carries a load to a dispersed point of need with no Soldier aboard or alongside, and reports its state to a supervising operator.

GPS waypoint following

Autonomy as an applique

Fitted to a platform already in service

The autonomy is a kit, not a new vehicle. An MPEP is disconnected from its remote-control compute unit and connected to the autonomy unit and sensor set — embedded GPU compute, LiDAR, visual and thermal cameras, and GPS/IMU — feeding a touchscreen operator display that carries active task, speed, trajectory, and vehicle and sensor health.

The stack is ROS/ROS 2-based and MOSA-aligned, with open APIs and no proprietary data lock. The same framework has been run on a diesel tracked forklift and an electric pallet jack.

  • Host platform delivered to Marine Corps organizations and to three Texas Air National Guard wings
  • Autonomy developed at the Texas A&M Center for Autonomous Vehicles and Sensor Systems under an Air Force SBIR, led by L5VEL founder Dr. Srikanth Saripalli, with host-platform partner Tracks North America
  • Carried forward by L5VEL with the same team and code baseline
