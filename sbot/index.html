Mobile manipulation

S-bot

A two-arm mobile manipulator built to work in spaces made for people. It finds objects that have moved, opens doors, and handles varied loads from a single trained system — no task-specific code.

S-bot retrieving a dropped object during development testing.

Where S-bots work

One platform, built for real environments

Home care

A supportive companion for elderly individuals and people with disabilities. S-bot helps maintain independence—picking up dropped items, assisting with light cleaning, and adapting to your unique home and needs.

Independence Companion Adaptive

Warehouse & logistics

Predictable mobile manipulation for demanding operations. Simple models and KISS engineering mean reliable performance that scales—whether moving inventory, organizing shelves, or handling varied objects.

Reliable Scalable KISS design

Infrastructure & industrial

Heavy-duty cleaning and maintenance at scale. From dam gates to lock systems, S-bots operate in environments that are hazardous or difficult for humans—keeping critical infrastructure safe and maintained.
